Our scarce present

Many see success in America as a brutal contest, where one must fight their fellow citizen for a finite pool of resources and opportunities. This instinct is understandable; America has not been producing what's needed to give everyone a shot at success. Americans' belief in government has been declining for decades, and a majority of young people lack hope for the future.

Our Abundant Future

There is a better way. We are working to make America the great nation of promise and plenty it was once thought to be. When young people work together to increase our energy supply, improve transportation, and defeat the entrenched interests that prevent home building, the possibility of a greater future comes into focus.
