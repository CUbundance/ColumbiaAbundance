Columbia University Chapter

Building an abundant future,
starting here at Columbia.

We're a student chapter of the national Abundance movement — organizing around the idea that solving our biggest challenges means building more: more housing, more clean energy, more infrastructure, faster.

What we're organizing around

The national Abundance agenda, applied locally.

H

Housing

Pushing for policy that makes it easier to build enough homes where people want to live.

E

Energy

Advocating for faster permitting and buildout of clean, abundant energy.

I

Infrastructure

Supporting projects and reforms that let public infrastructure get built on time.
